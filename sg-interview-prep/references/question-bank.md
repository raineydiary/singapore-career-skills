# 题库（按面试官三维评估模型组织）

用法：模拟面试选题时，保证三维都有覆盖；带 JD 时优先选与 KSA 关键词对应的题。每题标注了来源。

## 维度一：Can you do the job?（技能与胜任力）

- Tell us a bit more about yourself（FASS；NTU 指导：past-present-future 三段结构——背景 → 当前技能/焦点 → 为什么对这个岗位兴奋；不要复读简历，要把经历连到岗位要求；练熟但别背稿）
- 同题的变体谱系（FASS 提醒：从礼貌到攻击性措辞不等，本质同题）：What do you know about this role / our company? / What attracts you to our firm? / Why did you apply for this role? / What motivates you? / What work environment gets the best out of you? / **Why should we hire you?**
- How can you contribute?（FASS）
- How are you different from the competition?（FASS）
- What is your strength and weakness?（FASS 拆解见 answer-frameworks.md）
- What are your top three skills that make you suitable for this role?（NUS，3-Point 框架示范题）
- 经验类（TAFEP 合规题库）：What did you do in the last five years? / What were the challenges in your past experience as a [job title]? / Tell me about a specific area of responsibility that you have enjoyed most. / What have you learned from the jobs you have had?
- 分析能力类（TAFEP）：What would you do to increase productivity in your department? / 10 分钟准备一个"办公室火灾时你作为经理怎么办"的演示，你会讲哪些点？
- 技术/行业类（NBS）：考察逻辑推理过程 + 行业趋势认知 + commercial awareness（你的工作如何帮公司赚钱或省钱）

## 维度二：Do you love the job?（兴趣与动机）

- Why do you want this job?（FASS）
- What do you know about this role / company?（FASS，与维度一重叠——公司调研深度同时测能力与动机）
- Where else have you applied to and which would you choose?（FASS；诚实但策略性：表达对本岗位的明确偏好和理由）
- Why our company?（NTU 2026 范例思路：个人价值观/职业目标与公司方向对齐 + 点名让你兴奋的具体项目或倡议 + 描绘你在岗位上成功的样子）
- 热情与稳定性类（TAFEP）：What are the goals and objectives of your present company? Do you know our company's goals? / How long have you been looking for a new job? How long were you on your last job?
- 职业轨迹类（NUS，Past-Present-Future 框架示范题）：How has your past experience prepared you for this role and where do you see yourself in five years?

## 维度三：Can you fit in?（性格、价值观、操守）

- How would you manage conflicts with colleagues who may not agree with your work methods?（FASS）
- What would you do if you had to work with an uncooperative / unreasonable teammate / client?（FASS；TAFEP 合规版：supervisor 视角的下属不服管理题）
- How will your worst enemy describe you?（FASS）
- **If your boss or colleague was being unethical or breaking the rules, what would you do?**（FASS 拆解见 answer-frameworks.md——测价值观与因果推理，高频且高权重）
- 团队协作类（TAFEP）：Who are your team members in your present job? What is your working relationship with each of them? / Tell us the challenges you faced when working with a team.
- 诚信类（TAFEP）：What does integrity mean to you? / What would you do if you discovered a co-worker using the office phone for personal long-distance calls? / What are three adjectives others would use to describe you?
- 情绪管理类（TAFEP）：Give me an example of a crisis situation you were involved in. What did you do? / Tell me about a time when you were very angry at the workplace. What did you do? / Has there been a time when a person's anger was misdirected at you? How did you react?
- 适应力类（TAFEP）：How do you establish good working relationships with people? / If offered this position, how would you spend your first two weeks on the job? / Tell me about the most difficult job-related task you have faced.
- 自信与抗压（TAFEP）：When was the last time you were given a task beyond your scope of work? / What would you do if you were retrenched?
- 自我发展（TAFEP）：What training courses, seminars or workshops will you be attending in the next 12 months? / Do you think it is important to continue learning throughout one's lifetime? Why?
- 领导力（TAFEP）：If a subordinate is uncooperative and refuses to take instructions from you, what would you do? / Tell us of an incident of misconduct by a subordinate. What did you do? What was the outcome?

## 岗位条件确认类（TAFEP 合规，如实回答即可）

- 出差意愿：This job requires overseas travel more than 50% of the time. Would that be fine with you?
- 工时：The job often requires staying late to follow up on complaints. Would long hours be a problem?
- 体力要求：You are required to carry heavy objects (about 20kg). Can you handle that?
- **外语能力（合法且对双语者是送分题）**：You will be dealing with Chinese-speaking customers who cannot speak English. Can you communicate with them in Mandarin? / Can you reply to emails in Chinese? / Would you have problems speaking with Malay-speaking customers in Malay?

## 行为题万能句式（配 S.T.O.R.Y. 五故事使用）

"Tell me about a time when you…" + failed / led a team / faced conflicting deadlines / persuaded someone / made a mistake / went beyond your scope。每个 S.T.O.R.Y. 故事应对多个变体。

## ⚠️ 违规题清单（面试官不应问；模拟面试中 Claude 不问，但可应用户要求专项练习应对）

TAFEP 明文列举："How old are you?" / "What is your race and religion?" / "Are you married?" / "Are you planning to have children soon?" 以及涉及残障、家庭责任的提问。雇主若确需问敏感信息，有义务解释与岗位的关联。应对策略见 formats-etiquette.md。
